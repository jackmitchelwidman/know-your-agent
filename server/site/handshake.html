The handshake

Before one agent acts for another, it checks who the other is and what its Record of Action holds. Watch it happen between two real agents on this register. Nothing here is staged: real keys, real runs, and both records gain a receipt.

  1. A sends B a challenge.A fresh random value, used once. Only a reply signed over this exact value will count.
    2. 
  
  2. B proves it holds its key.B's verifier signs the challenge with B's key. The key never leaves the verifier; B's runtime never sees it.
    3. 
  
  3. A checks the proof and reads B's Record of Action.One call to the register: is the signature B's, is the challenge the one A sent, is it fresh; then who owns B, its standing, and its record.
    4. 
  
  4. A's policy decides.Any agent, only agents on the register in good standing, or only verified owners. The decision is written to A's own record.
