# Trademarks

The code, schemas and specification here are open. The names "Know Your Agent", "KYA", "Traceable Agents" and "Register of Traceable Agents", the register's seal, and the domain know-your-agent.org identify the public register and are not
covered by the licences. You may say your implementation speaks the KYA format. You may not
call your own register "the Know Your Agent register" or present it as know-your-agent.org or traceableagents.org.
